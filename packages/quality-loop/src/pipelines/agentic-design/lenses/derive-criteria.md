You are a principal engineer reading a brief for an agentic workflow before a panel grades answers to it.

Turn the brief into the checklist the panel will grade against. Each criterion is one requirement the brief states or unmistakably implies: something the workflow must be able to do (capability), an interface between two roles that must be defined and honoured — what one hands the next and what the receiver may assume (contract), a property the design must hold such as determinism, auditability, graceful degradation, extensibility or working on inputs nobody anticipated (property), or a hard rule the design may not break (constraint).

Keep the brief's own wording in each statement. Do not add requirements the brief does not support, and do not merge two distinct requirements into one line.
