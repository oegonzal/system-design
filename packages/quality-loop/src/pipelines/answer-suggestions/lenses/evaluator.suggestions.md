For each listed criterion decide pass, partial, fail or not_applicable, and cite your evidence — a field, field.1, a suggestion, field.1.2, or the summary. A grounding criterion passes when every record suggestion states only what its cited evidence states, every transfer cites recorded work and claims only how it carries over, and no suggestion claims what a claim_boundary binding it forbids; partial when one stretches its evidence; fail when one is labelled for what it does not rest on. A compliance criterion passes when the Suggesting policy line is honoured on every field it touches. A fit criterion passes when every suggestion answers its field's question as fully as the Suggesting policy asks and the evidence allows, can be submitted as it stands and differs from the others of its field; use not_applicable when no field is one the criterion could touch. Judge the suggestions on the goal alone: you measure how well they satisfy each criterion, and the policy decides.
