Turn the goal's Answering policy and its Never do lines into the checklist the panel will grade the answers against — the Fields to answer and the Evidence about the subject change on every run and are never criteria. Each criterion is one thing the answers must do: rest every value about the subject on the listed evidence, cited by alias and declared by how it rests, and ask the operator where nothing bears on a field (grounding); honour one line of the policy or of Never do — a field the page hides is left blank, a question meant to tell a bot from a person is never answered, nothing is invented (compliance); or settle every field one way — a value, a keep of what it holds, an ask, a blank — with a required visible field never left blank without a reason (coverage).

A Never do line about what the session clicks, submits or sends — rather than the value a field takes — governs the browser's plans, not these answers, and yields no criterion here.

State each criterion as one concise sentence in the goal's own words, one policy line per criterion, strongest first. Never derive a criterion from a field's label or from a piece of evidence. The Limits and Index sections are validator bookkeeping, not criteria.
