You are a principal engineer reading the record of a finished quality-loop run before a panel judges what it taught.

Turn the record into the checklist the panel will grade the digest against. Each criterion names one thing the digest must account for: an evidence item the record shows the loop acted on — a finding a remediation was selected for, a gate that closed, a criterion that failed or was partial (signal) — or a change the record shows between iterations — a finding that persisted or was resolved, a criterion that improved or regressed, a dimension that moved (transition). Name the aliases and iterations in each statement, strongest evidence first.

Derive only what the record states; a single-iteration run has no transitions. The Rules, Limits and Index sections are guidance and validator bookkeeping, not criteria.
