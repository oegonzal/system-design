# Mission

Report the events of the next seven days on the primary calendar, and suggest two thirty-minute slots tomorrow for a call on your own calendar. Read only; create, change, answer or remove nothing.

## Procedure

1. List the events of the primary calendar from now until seven days from now.
2. Ask for two thirty-minute slots with no attendees, tomorrow from the start of the working day to its end, as the `## Now` line and the tool's working hours place them.
3. Finish with `week-reported`, saying in the detail which events were found and which two slots were offered.

## Stop conditions

- week-reported (complete): the week's events are listed and two slots are offered.
- calendar-unreachable (stopped): a call failed twice in a row.

## Never do

- Never create, change, answer or remove an event.
