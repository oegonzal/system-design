Step 2 — Identify contracts Enfav could support
For each promising result, open the award details and look for:
Information	What matters
Recipient Name	The prime contractor you could approach
Award Description	Does the work involve software, AI, data or automation?
Award ID / PIID	Identifies the specific federal contract
Awarding Agency	Who the prime is serving
Period of Performance	Is the project currently active or starting soon?
Obligated Amount	Evidence of funds committed to the contract
Contract Type	Whether it's an actual funded task order or a broader contract vehicle

Important: A $100 million contract ceiling does not mean the contractor has received $100 million in funding. Look at obligations, active task orders and performance dates. USAspending distinguishes obligated amounts from other award values.

For Enfav, prioritize contracts involving AI/data integration, application modernization, workflow automation, document systems and testing.

Avoid contracts requiring large teams of cleared engineers unless a specifically unclassified, manageable work package is available.
Step 3 — Build a shortlist of 10 prime contractors
Research the awarded company and determine whether it has a small-business subcontracting program.

You can cross-reference your findings against the SBA Prime Contractor Directory.

The directory includes companies with federal subcontracting-plan requirements and contract information. Its published downloadable dataset is historical, so use USAspending to verify current programs.

Step 4 — Find the right person and become a supplier
After identifying a promising prime and program:
1. Visit the company's website.
2. Find Small Business, Suppliers, Subcontracting, or Doing Business With Us.
3. Register Enfav in its supplier portal if appropriate.
4. Identify the Small Business Liaison Officer (SBLO) or relevant program/capture manager.
5. Save the contact and contract information.
For example, these companies have established supplier pathways:
Company	Supplier pathway

Peraton	Supplier Intake
CACI	Small Business Office

These are potential business partners, not confirmed subcontract openings.
Step 5 — Contact primes with a specific value proposition
Your outreach should mention the actual contract or program you researched.

For example, instead of introducing Enfav merely as an AI company, explain that Enfav can support the prime's application modernization project through:
- AI document-processing and agentic workflow automation.
- RAG and enterprise knowledge retrieval.
- API integration and data engineering.
- Automated testing and CI/CD improvements.
Ask whether the program needs additional small-business engineering capacity and whether the contact can introduce you to the appropriate program manager.

Attach your one-page Enfav AI Capability Statement, rather than your personal résumé, unless the buyer specifically requests personnel qualifications.

For each promising program, investigate whether its requirements allow unclassified work by a U.S.-citizen engineer available for remote delivery and occasional travel.
Step 6 — Track leads and follow up
I recommend a very simple tracker:
Prime	Federal contract	Contact	Stage	Next action

Company B	Software development	Supplier manager	Registered	Send targeted introduction

Aim initially for 10 researched prime contractors, five relevant outreach contacts, and at least one qualified buyer conversation. These are activity targets, not predicted results.

After approximately five business days without a response, consider one concise follow-up, then continue prospecting rather than repeatedly emailing the same person.
